Who we help

Commercial cleaning

We help you win the recurring contracts that keep your crews busy, not just the one-off jobs.

Contracts, not callouts

Contract cleaning lives or dies on recurring commercial work. A one-off deep clean fills a day; a contract with an office, school or care home fills a diary for months and makes revenue predictable.

The hard part is getting in front of the people who actually award those contracts. Facilities managers and multi-site operators get pitched constantly, so most cleaning firms end up competing on price for whatever comes in. Reaching the right decision-maker first, with something relevant to their premises, changes that conversation.

Who we target

Outreach goes to the person who decides who cleans the building — typically the Facilities Manager, Office Manager, or School Business Manager.

Offices · Schools & Academy Trusts · Care Homes · Gyms & Leisure · Managed Buildings

What we do for commercial cleaning firms

  • Personalised outreach to business premises in your area that need regular, contracted cleaning.
  • Qualification handled for you, so only premises genuinely reviewing their cleaning cover reach your calendar.
  • Appointments booked directly — building a base of recurring contracts instead of one-off jobs.
