03 — How we qualify

What counts as a qualified appointment

You only pay for meetings that actually happen with someone worth meeting. An appointment counts when all of these hold:

  • The right organisation: a buyer of your service, in the area you cover
  • The decision-maker: or someone who can take the decision to them
  • A real need: work they want sorted within the next six months
  • A fit for you: within your capacity and above your minimum job size
  • The meeting happens: booked in your calendar and actually held
If a buyer doesn't show, we rebook them at no charge. If you think an appointment didn't meet the bar, tell us within two working days. No retainer and no minimum, and the first qualified appointment we book you is on us.
04 — Included for clients

Public-sector contracts, flagged to you

Councils, academy trusts and NHS bodies publish their contracts. We watch for the ones that match your trade and area, live tenders and contracts coming up for renewal, and pass them on as they appear, at no extra charge.
